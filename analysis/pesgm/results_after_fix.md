# Results after the constraint-layer fix

Generated by `10_after_fix.py` (reeds2 env). Every model's **stored** out-of-fold (OOF) predictions are used as they are; no model was retrained. All R² values are scored against the raw ReEDS targets, over the 66 system and 308 regional modelled outputs.

**Which numbers use the constraints.** Table III and the bucket and ranking figures use the **raw OOF scores (no constraints)** for every model. That is what the training pipeline's `summary.json` reports. Section D reports the effect of the constraint layer for the Random Forest (RF) only (Parts 2d, 2e, 2g, 2.9 and 3). There, "old" means the stored OOF predictions with no prediction-time rules, and "new" means after the fixed layer. The fixed layer holds constants, then clips, then rescales regional values to the system value, then applies the cost identity. Transmission corridors are rescaled only above their floor.
